# 📖 Overview

**Aryntra Sanchaya** is an offline-first life document management and preparation system that helps individuals and families securely preserve important records while staying prepared for significant life events.

Unlike conventional document storage applications, Sanchaya is designed around **preparedness rather than storage**.

Whether you're applying for a scholarship, renewing a passport, submitting tax documents, filing an insurance claim, or preparing for a government service, Sanchaya helps you organize, track, and prepare the required documents with confidence.

---

# ✨ Vision

> Build a trusted personal life-record system that empowers people to preserve important information today and stay prepared for tomorrow.

---

# 🎯 Mission

Create a secure, privacy-first platform that simplifies document management while reducing the stress of finding, organizing, and preparing important records when they matter most.

---

# 🌟 Core Principles

* 🔒 **Privacy by Design** — Your documents remain under your control.
* 💻 **Offline First** — Core functionality works without an internet connection.
* 📂 **Preparation over Storage** — Organization is only the first step.
* 🧩 **Need-Driven Evolution** — Every feature must solve a real user problem.
* 🏗️ **Architecture First** — Long-term maintainability over short-term convenience.

# 🏛️ Architecture

The project follows **Clean Architecture** to ensure long-term maintainability and technology independence.

```text
Presentation
      │
Application
      │
Domain
      │
Infrastructure
```

This separation keeps the business logic independent from frameworks, databases, and UI technologies.
